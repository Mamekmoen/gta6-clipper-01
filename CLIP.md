# The clip

Vertical 720x1280, 36 seconds. Cut in this session from Rockstar's official
GTA VI Launch Trailer (Oct 15, 2026), downloaded from Rockstar's own CDN
(media-rockstargames-com.akamaized.net/VI/downloads/videos/GTAVI_Launch_Trailer/).

Hook (0-24s, original trailer audio): the red sports car, Lucia in the
convertible, the Vice City street, Jason & Lucia's sunset embrace, the night
sniper — vertical centre-crop of the 4K trailer.

Pitch (24-36s): the OUT NOW skyline end-card, darkened, with the make.fun
pitch as on-screen text and an English voiceover (TTS, in-session):
"GTA 6 is out now. But here's the real cheat code. Make clips like this
with AI, and earn money on make.fun." On screen: MAKE CLIPS LIKE THIS /
WITH AI AND / EARN MONEY ON make.fun / make.fun/bounty/gta6-clipper-01.

uploaded to YouTube by the channel owner (KING DRAMA) and its address goes
